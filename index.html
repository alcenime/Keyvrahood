THE KEYVRA PROTOCOL
STAKE. POWER. EPOCH. REWARDS.

KEYVRA runs in epochs. Every epoch creates a new staking cycle where staked KEYVRA becomes staking power.

Every 1 KEYVRA represents 1 TH of staking power.

At the end of an epoch, rewards are shared according to staking power.

KEYVRA VALUE
1 KEYVRA = 1 TH

Every KEYVRA staked represents 1 TH of staking power.

THE MORE YOU STAKE. THE GREATER YOUR SHARE.
