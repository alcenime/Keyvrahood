WHAT IS KEYVRA?

KEYVRA is an epoch-based staking protocol built on Robinhood Chain.

Users stake KEYVRA to gain staking power and participate in recurring epoch reward cycles.

Each epoch creates a new opportunity for stakers to participate in the protocol's rewards.

Because 1 KEYVRA = 1 TH, staking power can be represented directly by the amount of KEYVRA staked.

The larger your share of total staking power, the larger your share of the epoch reward pool.

STAKING

KEYVRA staking allows users to stake KEYVRA and participate in epoch-based rewards.

Users can stake KEYVRA when staking is live.

Users can unstake according to the protocol's staking rules.

EPOCH SYSTEM

KEYVRA organizes staking into recurring epochs.

Each epoch has:

  • A beginning
  • A staking period
  • An end
  • A reward distribution
